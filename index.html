Privacy Policy for Magnum Rider

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Magnum Rider is a racing game for Android, published under the package name com.gorga.racing. This policy explains what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

Who is responsible

Magnum Rider is developed and operated by Johan Nevstedt, an individual developer based in Sweden. For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), I am the data controller for the information described below.

Contact: magnum.rider.developer@gmail.com

What the game collects

When you sign in with Google Play Games, the game receives your Play Games player identifier and your Play Games display name. It does not receive your real name, your email address, or your Google account password. Everything else listed here is generated by playing the game.

What the game does not collect

  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera access.
  • No advertising identifier — the game contains no advertising.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No third-party trackers of any kind.

The game requests only two Android permissions: internet access and the ability to check whether a network connection exists. Both are needed to reach the game server.

Why this data is collected

All of it exists to make the game work: to save your progress between sessions, to show leaderboards, to let other players race tracks you have built, and to match players in battles. None of it is used for advertising, profiling or marketing, and none of it is sold.

Under GDPR, the legal basis is the performance of a contract — providing the game you chose to install and sign in to. Where that basis does not apply, the basis is your consent, given when you sign in with Google Play Games, which you can withdraw by asking for your data to be deleted.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. The data is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, analytics providers, or any other third party.

The one external service the game uses is Google Play Games Services, which authenticates you and provides your player ID and display name. Google's own handling of that is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Where it is stored, and how it is protected

Game data is stored in a database on a server operated personally by the developer, not on a commercial cloud service. The database is not reachable from the internet: it accepts connections only from the machine it runs on, and the application connects to it using an account restricted to this game's data.

Please be aware: the connection between the game on your device and the game server is a direct network connection that is not encrypted. Your player ID, display name and game progress travel over that connection in a readable form. Someone able to observe traffic on the same network could in principle read them. No passwords are transmitted, because sign-in is handled entirely by Google. Encrypting this connection is a known improvement that has not yet been made.

How long it is kept

Your player record and the tracks you have built are kept for as long as the game operates, so that your progress is there when you return. If you ask for deletion, the data is removed.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or EEA, GDPR gives you the right to request a copy of the data held about you, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have your data deleted, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to receive it in a portable form. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority; in Sweden this is the Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).

These requests are free, and I will respond within one month.

Deleting your data

To have your player record, progress and created tracks deleted, email magnum.rider.developer@gmail.com from the address associated with your Google account, or include your Play Games display name so the right record can be found. Deletion is permanent — your progress cannot be recovered afterwards.

Children

Magnum Rider is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data through the game, contact me and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with an updated date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the game's Play Store listing.
